# Privacy & Zero-Identity Disclosure Model

## Why Communication ID?
Traditional messengers require revealing personal phone numbers or email addresses, exposing users to SIM swapping, identity harvesting, spam, and doxxing.

**MESSAGER** solves this through a randomized 8-digit **Communication ID** (`58392147`):
- Other users only ever see your Communication ID and display name.
- Your Google email, real identity, and telephone number remain 100% private.

## Granular Privacy Controls

Users can configure preferences in **Settings -> Privacy**:

1. **Messaging Requests**:
   - `Everyone` (Any user with your ID can connect)
   - `Contacts Only`
   - `Nobody`
2. **Thoughts Visibility**:
   - `Everyone`
   - `Contacts Only`
   - `Close Friends Only`
   - `Nobody`
3. **Profile Photo & Presence**:
   - Customizable visibility for avatar, online indicator, and last-seen timestamps.
4. **Read Receipts**:
   - Can be toggled on or off at will.
5. **Close Friends Isolation**:
   - Adding a user to Close Friends is completely silent. Other users cannot see if they are in your Close Friends list or view your list.
